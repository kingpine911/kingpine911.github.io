The Radama Islands, a wild paradise

South of Nosy Be, the Radama Islands form an unspoilt archipelago of crystal-clear waters and pristine beaches. Renowned for their coral reefs, exceptional biodiversity and sheltered anchorages, they are an ideal setting for diving, snorkelling and discovering Malagasy nature.

6 days / 5 nights10 divesItinerary adjusted to the weather
  1. D1

    Heading for Nosy Iranja

    Boarding our Lagoon 450 catamaran (45 feet). The cruise sets off for Nosy Iranja: 5 hours' sailing. Anchorage in the bay.

  2. D2

    On to Antanimora

    Morning: 2 dives offshore. Afternoon: 5 hours' sailing to the island of Antanimora. Anchorage, visit of the island and its inhabitants. Night dive.

  3. D3

    Kalakajoro island

    Morning: 2 dives offshore, then 3 hours' sailing to Kalakajoro island. Anchorage, landing and visit of the island.

  4. D4

    Back to Nosy Iranja

    Morning: 2 dives. 4 hours' sailing to reach Nosy Iranja. Anchorage and visit of the island.

  5. D5

    Antsoha and Sakatia

    Morning: 2 dives. 1 hour's sailing to Antsoha island, and visit. In the afternoon, on to Sakatia, with a visit of the island.

  6. D6

    Return to Nosy Be

    Morning: 2 dives. Return and disembarkation in Nosy Be around 3 pm.

The Mitsio, a tropical adventure off Nosy Be

The Mitsio archipelago is one of Madagascar's most beautiful destinations. With its spectacular rock pinnacles, white-sand beaches and turquoise lagoons, this paradise offers exceptional diving and abundant marine life: turtles, rays, schools of tropical fish and sometimes unforgettable pelagic encounters.

6 days / 5 nights10 divesItinerary adjusted to the weather
  1. D1

    Heading for the Mitsio

    Boarding our Lagoon 450 catamaran (45 feet). The cruise sets off for the Mitsio: 6 hours' sailing. Anchorage at Tsarabanjina.

  2. D2

    The basalt organ pipes

    Arrival in the Mitsio archipelago. Morning: 2 dives offshore. Afternoon: 2 hours' sailing past the basalt organ pipes. Anchorage in Grande Mitsio bay, visit of the island and its inhabitants.

  3. D3

    Ankarea island

    Morning: 2 dives offshore. Landing on Ankarea island. Anchorage at Marimbe.

  4. D4

    Ampanisoha

    Morning: 2 dives. 2 hours' sailing to reach Ampanisoha. Anchorage and visit of the island.

  5. D5

    Heading for Sakatia

    Morning: 2 dives. Departure for Sakatia, about 5 hours' sailing, then anchorage.

  6. D6

    Return to Nosy Be

    Morning: 2 dives. Return and disembarkation in Nosy Be around 3 pm.

A paradise for non-divers too

You don't need to dive to fall in love with the Radama and Mitsio islands. Picture yourself waking up each morning in a new bay of turquoise water, surrounded by wild islands and deserted beaches that only a boat can reach. Long white-sand beaches invite you to swim, stroll at the water's edge and discover unspoilt nature, far from mass tourism.

6 days / 5 nightsSnorkelling, islands and villagesItinerary adjusted to the weather
  1. D1

    Nosy Tanikely

    Boarding our Lagoon 450 catamaran (45 feet). Departure at 9 am for Tanikely, a marine reserve: snorkelling and a stroll on the beach. Around 3 pm, on to Russian Bay for the night's anchorage.

  2. D2

    Nosy Iranja

    2 hours' sailing to Nosy Iranja: an authentic village, exceptional beaches and two islands joined by a sandbar. Anchorage at Nosy Iranja.

  3. D3

    The Kalakajoro islands

    Sailing to the Kalakajoro islands. Landing for a walk through lush vegetation, then a visit to the fishing village. Anchorage for the night.

  4. D4

    Antanimora, the bird island

    Visit of Antanimora, home to thousands of birds. Visit of the village, swimming. Anchorage at Baramahamay.

  5. D5

    Back to Nosy Iranja

    Stopover at Nosy Iranja, the turtle island, and its beautiful beach. Anchorage in Russian Bay.

  6. D6

    Antsoha, the lemur island

    On to Antsoha, the lemur island, then sailing to Ankazoberavina: a walk and the beach. Return to Nosy Be.
