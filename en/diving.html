Our diving services

Every dive is an invitation to travel

Off the north-west coast of Madagascar, Nosy Be reveals an unspoilt world where the beauty of the ocean shows itself in all its splendour: an immersion in a world vibrant with colour, life and emotion.

We take you to explore the finest sites of the archipelago: lush coral reefs, dizzying drop-offs, coral gardens and passes teeming with marine life. You will meet sea turtles, rays, schools of tropical fish and, depending on the season, impressive whale sharks or majestic humpback whales.

More than a simple boat trip, we offer an authentic, personal and friendly experience. Our passion for the ocean, our knowledge of the sites and our commitment to preserving the marine environment make every dive a special moment.
