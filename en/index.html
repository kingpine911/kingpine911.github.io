The centre

More than thirty years exploring the seabed of Nosy Be

Based on the "perfume island" since 1990, Oceane's Dream is one of Madagascar's historic dive clubs. Our team of instructors looks after first-time divers and seasoned explorers alike, with the safety and comfort you would expect from the finest destinations in the Indian Ocean.

Turtles, manta rays, whale sharks in season, coral gardens: the sites around Nosy Be, Nosy Tanikely and Nosy Iranja rank among the most beautiful islands in the world. We know them by heart.

  • Certified instructors, guiding in French and English
  • Small groups to preserve the quality of every dive
  • Recent, well-maintained equipment in every size
  • A respectful approach to reefs and local wildlife
