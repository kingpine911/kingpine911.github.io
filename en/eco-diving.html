Madagascar Attitude & Oceane's Dream

A different way to dive in Madagascar

An eco-responsible diving experience, where every dive can help protect the ocean.

What we believe

Discovering the seabed goes hand in hand with protecting it

We believe that discovering the underwater wonders of Madagascar goes hand in hand with protecting them. That is why, in partnership with Madagascar Attitude, we have developed an eco-responsible diving programme that lets everyone play a part in preserving the ocean.

Guided by our marine biologist, our divers have the opportunity to take part in hands-on conservation work.

Madagascar Attitude

In the field

Four hands-on actions

Beyond discovering Madagascar's exceptional seabed, we offer our guests a unique experience in which every dive can contribute to protecting the environment.

01

Coral restoration

Helping to rehabilitate coral reefs, the true lungs of marine biodiversity.

02

Beach clean-ups

Collecting litter to preserve coastal ecosystems and the natural beauty of our shoreline.

03

Mangrove replanting

Planting young mangroves, essential for protecting the coast and for the reproduction of many marine species.

04

Tackling ghost fishing

Recovering drifting nets and abandoned fishing gear that keep trapping and killing marine life.

Dive. Discover. Protect.

Together, let's turn our passion for the ocean into a concrete commitment for future generations.
