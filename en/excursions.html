Far north tour

Diego-Suarez in 8 days and 7 nights

From the lagoon of the Emerald Sea to the Tsingy of Ankarana, by 4×4, on foot, by dhow and by outrigger canoe: wild Madagascar, day after day.

€1,260per person
Minimum 2 participantsNights at the La Belle Aventure hotel, the Kozubé hotel and a guest room at the Millot plantation.
Ask for dates
  1. D1

    From Nosy Be to Diego-Suarez

    Departure at 6 am from Ambatoloaka by boat to the mainland: a 1-hour crossing. Arrival in Ankify, then the drive to Diego-Suarez along the RN6 by 4×4. Around noon, lunch at Snack-17, an original and tasty meal. Arrival at 4.30 pm in Diego-Suarez, the city of a thousand colours. Night at the La Belle Aventure hotel, dinner on your own.

  2. D2

    The Three Bays coastline

    A guided hike: Sakalava Bay, Pigeon Bay, Dunes Bay. Return by car to visit the Cap Miné lighthouse and the former French military bases, before relaxing on Orangea beach. Lunch (not included) on Ramena beach. In the afternoon, back to Diego and a tour of the town.

  3. D3

    Emerald Sea or Amber Mountain

    Aboard a wooden dhow, visit the enchanting lagoon of the Emerald Sea. Landing on Suarez islet for lunch under a thatched shelter, then back under sail around 4 pm.OrDrive to Amber Mountain and Joffreville: waterfall, lake, tropical forest, wildlife and plants. Night at the La Belle Aventure hotel.

  4. D4

    French Mountain

    Departure for French Mountain for a 2.5-hour hike along the trails. Lunch at the La Casa restaurant. Visit of the picturesque town and its market. Night at the La Belle Aventure hotel.

  1. D5

    Red Tsingy and the Sacred Lake

    The adventure continues along the RN6 to the Red Tsingy and the Sacred Lake. Lunch at Violette's. Night and dinner at the Kozubé hotel.

  2. D6

    Ankarana park

    Visit of Ankarana park and guided tour of the grey Tsingy. Lunch in the park, in the company of the lemurs. Night at the Kozubé hotel.

  3. D7

    The Bons Pères waterfall

    Departure for the Bons Pères waterfall across the Ambilobe region and its coffee, cocoa and ylang-ylang plantations. Outrigger canoe trip on the Ramena river, up to the waterfall. Picnic, then back to Ambanja. Night and dinner in a guest room at the Millot plantation.

  4. D8

    The Millot plantation

    Visit of the Millot plantation, master chocolate maker. Late morning, boarding a speedboat for the return to Ambatoloaka.

Our prices are indicative: tailor-made stays on request.
