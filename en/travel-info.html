Before you go

The essentials

Entry requirements

  • Passport valid for 6 months after your return date
  • 2-month visa, payable on arrival: about €35

Getting there

  • From France, international flights via Antananarivo, Addis Ababa or Réunion
  • Once there, getting around is easy by tuk-tuk or taxi

In your dive bag

  • Medical certificate less than 12 months old
  • Certification card
  • Dive logbook
  • Dive insurance

Health

  • No compulsory vaccinations
  • Preventive malaria treatment recommended, on your doctor's advice
  • Best of all: protect yourself well against mosquitoes
  • Beware of stomach upsets caused by the change of diet
  • Drink bottled water
  • Good medical facilities, well-stocked pharmacies
  • For divers: a working hyperbaric chamber

Good to know

  • Languages: French, English, Malagasy
  • Currency: the ariary. €1 is worth 4,500 to 4,800 Ar on average
  • Time difference with France: + 2 h in winter, + 1 h in summer
  • Electricity: 220 volts

FAQ

Your questions, our answers

When is the best time to dive in Nosy Be?
Nosy Be is a year-round diving destination thanks to its warm waters and rich marine biodiversity. The best period generally runs from April to November, during the dry season, with excellent underwater visibility and calmer seas.
When can you see humpback whales?
Between July and September, divers may be lucky enough to watch humpback whales during their migration.
And whale sharks?
From November to December, the presence of whale sharks draws many enthusiasts.
Can you dive during the rainy season?
Yes. The rainy season, from January to March, brings a warmer sea but sometimes reduced visibility because of the rainfall. Whatever period you choose, Nosy Be promises exceptional encounters with corals, turtles, rays and countless tropical fish.
What is the water temperature?
It is constant all year round: 26 to 29 °C.
What documents do I need to dive?
A medical certificate less than 12 months old, your certification card, your dive logbook and dive insurance.
Is there a hyperbaric chamber in Nosy Be?
Yes. A hyperbaric chamber is located just 1 kilometre from the centre. It is run by doctors specialised in hyperbaric medicine.
Do I need a visa for Madagascar?
A 2-month visa is paid on arrival: about €35. Your passport must be valid for 6 months after your return date.
