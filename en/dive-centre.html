The Oceane's Dream centre

A few steps from the beach, in the heart of Ambatoloaka

Oceane's Dream invites you to discover the most beautiful dive sites of the Indian Ocean in exceptional comfort, safety and quality.

Our centre enjoys an ideal location: a few steps from the beach, the hotels, the restaurants and the island's main tourist facilities. This prime position gives quick access to the best dive spots of the archipelago.

The fleet

A modern, high-performance fleet

Our fleet suits every kind of underwater exploration, from day trips to expeditions and cruises lasting several days.

  • A comfortable catamaran, specially fitted out for dive cruises, to reach the most unspoilt and remote sites of the archipelago in the best conditions.
  • Two speedboats, each fitted with 200 hp Suzuki engines, for fast, safe and comfortable transfers to the dive sites.

Equipment

Brand-new, high-quality equipment

Because comfortable, reliable equipment is essential to a great experience, Oceane's Dream provides its divers with an entirely new set of gear.

Our centre is equipped to welcome up to 30 divers at the same time, with:

  • 65 steel tanks of 12 litres
  • recent, regularly serviced diving equipment
  • professional logistics, suited to beginners and the most experienced divers alike
See dive packs and equipment rental
Safety

Safety first

Safety is at the heart of our commitment. Our centre benefits from a particularly reassuring medical environment, with a hyperbaric chamber located just 1 kilometre away.

This facility is run by doctors specialised in hyperbaric medicine, ensuring fast and effective care if needed.

All our procedures, our equipment and our organisation are designed to give our guests a calm and safe diving experience.

About us

More than thirty years of history in Nosy Be

Oceane's Dream is Madagascar's first dive club, based from the very start in the centre of the little village of Ambatoloaka.

  1. 1990

    The founding

    The club is founded by Laurent Duriez and Eric Mercier.

  2. 2000

    The team grows

    Gilles Mercier and Patrick Sahuquet join the adventure.

  3. Today

    A 100% Malagasy team
